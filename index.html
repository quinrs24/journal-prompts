Keep your writing safe

Your writing is saved in this browser, not uploaded to GitHub. Prompts sync to your Google Sheet when connected. Journal text is sent to Google only if you select Append to Google Doc. Browser storage is not encrypted; someone with access to your unlocked device and browser profile may be able to read it. Clearing website data or using private browsing can erase entries. iPhone and Mac journals are separate.

Back up your journal

Export an encrypted backup protected by a passphrase. Store the file somewhere safe and remember the passphrase: it cannot be recovered. Backups are created on your device.

Restore a backup

Restoring replaces this device's current journal, including its prompts and entries. Export a backup first if you want to keep what's here.

Privacy controls

Private by design, but not password-locked. Back up regularly, especially before clearing browser data or changing phones.
